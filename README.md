## 🚀 Project 1: Customer Churn Prediction

### 📌 Business Problem
A telecom company wants to understand **why customers are leaving** and what factors drive churn, so they can take proactive retention actions.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Python (Pandas):** Data cleaning & preprocessing
- **SQL:** Business logic & metric calculation
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards & visualizations

### 🔧 Data Cleaning (Python)
- Removed duplicate records
- Handled missing/null values
- Corrected data types to ensure accurate analysis

### 📊 SQL Analysis
| Query | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Churn Rate | Percentage of customers who left |
| Internet Service Effect | How internet service type impacts churn |
| Contract Effect | Impact of contract type on churn |
| Dependents Effect | Whether having dependents affects churn likelihood |
| Payment Method Effect | Which payment methods correlate with higher churn |

### 📈 Power BI Dashboard
- Churn Rate Percentage
- Internet Service Effect on Churn
- Contract Type Effect on Churn
- Dependents Effect on Churn
- Payment Method Effect on Churn

### 💡 Key Insights
- 🚪 **Churn Rate:** [X%] of customers have left the service
- 🌐 **Internet Service:** Customers with [Fiber Optic / DSL] show [higher/lower] churn rates
- 📄 **Contract Type:** [Month-to-month] contracts have significantly higher churn than [1-year / 2-year] contracts
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Dependents:** Customers [with/without] dependents are [more/less] likely to churn
- 💳 **Payment Method:** [Electronic check] users show the highest churn tendency

### 💡 Recommendations
1. **Target Month-to-Month Customers:** Offer incentives to switch to longer-term contracts
2. **Payment Method Review:** Investigate why electronic check users churn more — consider promoting auto-pay or credit card options
3. **Family Plans:** Create bundled offers for customers with dependents to increase stickiness
4. **Internet Service Quality:** Address service quality issues for high-churn internet types

## 🚀 Project 2: E-Commerce Sales Analysis

### 📌 Business Problem
An online retail business needs to understand **customer behavior, revenue patterns, and geographic performance** to optimize sales strategy.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Python (Pandas):** Data cleaning & type correction
- **SQL:** Business queries & aggregations
- **Power BI:** Executive dashboard & KPI tracking

### 🔧 Data Cleaning (Python)
- Cleaned raw transactional data
- Corrected data types for dates, prices, and quantities

### 📊 SQL Analysis
| Query | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Top 10 Customers | Identified highest-value customers by total sales |
| Revenue by Country | Geographic revenue distribution |
| Monthly Growth | Month-over-month revenue trend analysis |

### 📈 Power BI Dashboard
- Top 10 Customers by Total Sales
- Customer Count by Category (based on number of transactions)
- Revenue by Month
- Total Revenue (KPI)
- Count of Orders (KPI)
- Count of Customers (KPI)
- Average Order Value (KPI)
- Total Revenue by Country

### 💡 Key Insights
- 🏆 **Top Customers:** The top 10 customers generated [X%] of total revenue
- 🌍 **Geographic Performance:** [Country X] generated the highest revenue, while [Country Y] showed strong growth potential
- 📈 **Monthly Trends:** Revenue peaked in [Month] with [X%] growth compared to previous month
- 🛒 **Average Order Value:** AOV stands at [$X], indicating [opportunity for upselling / healthy basket size]

### 💡 Recommendations
1. **VIP Program:** Launch a loyalty program for the top 10% of customers to increase retention
2. **Geographic Expansion:** Increase marketing investment in high-performing countries
3. **Seasonal Strategy:** Prepare inventory and campaigns for peak months identified in the analysis
4. **Upselling Opportunity:** Focus on increasing AOV through bundle offers and cross-selling
